# IceCube-Gen2 LOM-16 for PPC

Written by `examples/06_fit_lom_shape.py` from public sources only. Citations and the
digitization are described in `examples/data/lom/README.md`.

- `om.conf`: shape parameters fitted to the effective area against zenith angle at 400 nm.
  The RMS residual is 1.7% of the mean area. The PMT angles are effective values, not the
  mechanical tilts, and the azimuthal response was not fitted.
- `om.wv_171.0`: wavelength [nm] and direction-averaged effective area [cm^2].

These are not IceCube's own PPC files. They have not been validated against an IceCube
simulation of hit yields or times.
